About

Hey, I'm João!

I've always been passionate about technology and audio and made it a career. I studied electrical engineering in Brazil (with a focus on electronics and DSP), moved to Montreal to do a master's degree in auditory modeling, and ended up working at MILA on one of the first neural speech synthesis models. I then spent 6 years at NVIDIA working on applications of AI to speech and audio, such as Riva, Fugatto, and Music Flamingo.

These days I work on Neural Amp Modeler at Tone3000, including the A2 architecture and running neural amp models on embedded hardware (see my write-up and projects). I also take on independent consulting work on all things AI and audio. If you'd like to chat about either, feel free to reach out!
