Projects

Neural Amp Modeler @ Tone3000

  • nam-pedal: example firmware running NAM in real time on the Electrosmith Daisy Seed (ARM Cortex-M7), meant as a blueprint for porting NAM to embedded targets.
  • nam-binary-loader: .namb, a compact binary model format and loader for memory-constrained devices.
  • nam-inference-benchmarks: micro-benchmarks for NAM inference operations on desktop and Cortex-M7.
  • NeuralAmpModelerCore contributions: inline GEMM and embedded optimizations and decoupling model construction from JSON parsing.
  • Write-up: Bringing NAM A2 to Embedded Hardware.

Speech and audio AI research

  • Fugatto and Music Flamingo (NVIDIA): generative audio and music understanding models.
  • Riva (NVIDIA): speech AI SDK.
  • Char2Wav (MILA): one of the first end-to-end neural speech synthesis models.
  • SRMRpy: Python implementation of the SRMR speech quality and intelligibility metric, from my master's work on auditory modeling.

Side projects

  • Bebhionn: a vertical tracker for the Sega Saturn's YMF292 sound chip, running MAME's SCSP emulation in the browser via WebAssembly. Write-up coming soon.
